    The FastDL extension was not found. Install it once:
    1. Open a new tab and paste chrome://extensions
      (a page is not allowed to open it for you)
    2. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
    3. Click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder inside app:
    4. On its card click Details and turn on Allow access to file URLs. Without this the app cannot see the extension.
    5. Come back here and click Check again.
    Already installed? Click its reload arrow ⟳ on chrome://extensions (older copies cannot talk to the app), and check step 4.
